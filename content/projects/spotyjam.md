## Overview

A collaborative playlist experience where guests can suggest songs anonymously without needing a Spotify account.

## Problem

Music is social, but contribution flows often assume every guest has an account, the right app and permission to control the playlist. A gathering needs a lower-friction path.

## Context

An independent product experiment combining a shared physical setting with a lightweight web interaction and Spotify integration.

## My role

Product design and development across the guest experience, integration boundaries and experiment design.

## Constraints

- Guests should be able to participate without Spotify accounts.
- Suggestions need to coexist with host control.
- The flow must be quick enough to use during a social event.

## Approach

Separate guest participation from playlist ownership and reduce contribution to a short, anonymous interaction.

## Key decisions

- Keep authentication with the host side of the experience.
- Design guest participation for a shared, temporary context.
- Use integration capability in service of the social experience.
