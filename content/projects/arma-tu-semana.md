## Overview

A simple, browser-based weekly planner designed to let people start planning without accounts or unnecessary setup.

## Problem

Many planning tools make a lightweight personal routine begin with registration, configuration and feature decisions. The product starts from a simpler question: how quickly can someone shape the week in front of them?

## Context

An independent product exploring frictionless onboarding, focused scope and browser-first utility.

## My role

Product design and development across the concept, interaction model, implementation and self-hosting decisions.

## Constraints

- No account should be required to begin.
- The core workflow should remain understandable on a small screen.
- Added capability should not dilute the focused planning experience.

## Approach

Make the first useful action immediate, keep the weekly model legible and allow the browser to carry the experience without unnecessary service dependencies.

## Key decisions

- Start with the week, not account creation.
- Prefer direct manipulation and clear defaults.
- Keep deployment and operation small enough for an independent product.
