## Overview

Reassessing an inherited alerting system to reduce ignored notifications and route actionable signals into the workflows teams actually use.

## Problem

Alerts lose value when they are noisy, lack ownership or arrive outside the places where teams coordinate work. The challenge is as much about product behavior and workflow design as monitoring technology.

## My role

Product strategy and workflow design, bringing operational needs, system behavior and team practices into the same problem definition.

## Constraints

- Improve trust without interrupting essential operational coverage.
- Work with established tools and team habits.
- Avoid unsupported claims about notification or incident reduction.

## Approach

Examine which signals lead to action, how ownership is determined and where teams already coordinate. Treat routing, context and feedback as part of alert quality.

## Key decisions

- Measure usefulness by the action an alert enables.
- Route signals into existing workflows where practical.
- Make tuning and ownership ongoing product responsibilities.
