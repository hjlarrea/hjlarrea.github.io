Overview

A Spanish-language expense-sharing application designed to make splitting the cost of gatherings simple and understandable.

Problem

Shared expenses become confusing when different people pay for different parts of a gathering. The useful answer is not just arithmetic; it is a clear explanation of who owes what.

Context

An independent product focused on a familiar collaborative task and an accessible, responsive experience.

My role

Product design and development, including the user flow, API shape and responsive interface.

Constraints

  • Keep the language and results understandable.
  • Support real gathering expenses without making setup burdensome.
  • Preserve a coherent experience across phone and desktop screens.

Approach

Model the minimum information needed to calculate a fair split, then present the result as a small set of understandable actions.

Key decisions

  • Design in Spanish around the users’ vocabulary.
  • Keep calculations behind a clear explanation.
  • Treat responsive behavior as part of the core workflow.
