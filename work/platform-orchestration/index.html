Overview

A product approach to simplifying complex infrastructure and deployment workflows without creating another disconnected engineering standard.

Problem

Infrastructure workflows can accumulate specialized steps, disconnected tooling and hidden operational knowledge. The product challenge is to reduce that burden while preserving the flexibility engineers need.

My role

Product strategy and technical design, connecting platform capabilities with the workflows and decisions experienced by engineering teams.

Constraints

  • Preserve access to operational detail and escape hatches.
  • Avoid imposing a new standard disconnected from existing workflows.
  • Describe confidential work without internal names or unsupported claims.

Approach

Begin with user journeys, identify repeated decisions and friction, and treat orchestration as a product interface rather than a layer that hides infrastructure completely.

Key decisions

  • Make workflows understandable before automating them.
  • Keep system state and feedback visible.
  • Introduce enablement incrementally instead of requiring a platform rewrite.
