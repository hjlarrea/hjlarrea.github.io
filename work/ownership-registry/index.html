Ownership Registry

02

Making ownership of services and technical resources discoverable through a lightweight, API-first system.

Role
Product strategy and technical design
Type
Professional case study
Status
Case study

Areas

  • Internal tools
  • Service ownership
  • Platform clarity
  • Product discovery

Overview

A lightweight, API-first system for answering who owns a service, repository, Kubernetes namespace or Jira project.

Problem

When ownership information is fragmented, people lose time finding the right team and operational work is harder to route. A registry must make ownership discoverable without becoming another stale inventory.

Context

Content needed: Add anonymized context about the ownership workflow and its users.

My role

Product strategy and technical design, including problem framing, resource relationships and the shape of a useful ownership interface.

Constraints

  • Work across different resource types and existing systems of record.
  • Keep maintenance effort low enough for information to remain trustworthy.
  • Avoid exposing confidential organization details.

Approach

Model the smallest useful ownership relationships, expose them through an API-first interface and design around the questions users actually ask.

Key decisions

  • Prefer a lightweight registry over a broad catalog mandate.
  • Separate ownership data from the interfaces that consume it.
  • Make missing or ambiguous ownership visible instead of masking it.
