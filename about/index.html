About

Product thinking grounded in technical reality

I’m a Technical Product Manager with a background in infrastructure engineering. I work where product strategy, platform engineering and developer experience meet.

I remain technically hands-on because strong product decisions depend on understanding the systems, constraints and trade-offs beneath the interface. I approach platforms as products and force multipliers: their value comes from making difficult work clearer, safer and easier to repeat.

My focus is reducing friction and cognitive load while connecting technical constraints with user and organizational needs. Outside professional work, I build independent products and experiments. I use ColoLabs to publish ideas, tutorials, videos and experiments.

How I work

  • Begin with the workflow and user problem. Understand how work happens before choosing a solution.
  • Question the abstraction. A new layer must remove more complexity than it creates.
  • Enable incrementally. Prefer useful steps and observable adoption over large platform rewrites.
  • Design the feedback loop. Visibility, feedback and escape hatches are part of the experience.
  • Prototype to clarify. Make decisions tangible early, then learn from real use.
